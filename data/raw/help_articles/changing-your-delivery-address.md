# Changing your delivery address

## Before dispatch
While the order shows **Processing**, sign in, open the order, and choose **Edit
address**. This is usually possible for about two hours after ordering.

## After dispatch
We cannot change the address once the courier has the parcel. Your options:

- Use the courier's own redirect service via the tracking link. Most allow a
  redirect to a neighbour or a collection point.
- Let the delivery fail. After two attempts it returns to us and we refund minus
  the original delivery charge, and you can reorder.

## Changing the address on your account
**My account** then **Addresses**. This changes the default for future orders
only and does not affect orders already placed.

## Billing address
The billing address must match your card. A mismatch is a common cause of
declines. See *Payment methods*.

## Subscriptions
Changing your default address updates future subscription deliveries, but not one
already dispatched.
