# Replacing the water filter

Applies to the K7 and K7 Pro.

## When to replace it
Every three months, or sooner in a hard water area if the flow through the spout
slows. Our subscription sends one automatically on that schedule. See *Filter
subscription terms*.

## Fitting a new one
1. Open the lid and pull the filter holder straight up out of its slot behind
   the spout.
2. Squeeze the tabs and slide the old cartridge out.
3. Rinse the new cartridge under a cold tap for 30 seconds.
4. Slide it into the holder and push the holder back down until level.

## First boil after fitting
Fill to maximum, boil, and discard that water. Carbon fines from a new cartridge
make the first boil cloudy. This is harmless but tastes dusty.

## Running the kettle without a filter
The kettle works normally without one. The filter catches limescale flakes; it
does not soften the water or affect boiling.

## Do not wash cartridges
A cartridge cannot be cleaned or reused. Once the carbon is exhausted it must be
replaced. Opened cartridges cannot be returned. See *Items that cannot be
returned*.
