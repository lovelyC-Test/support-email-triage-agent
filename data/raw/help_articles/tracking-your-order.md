# Tracking your order

## Where to find the tracking number
Sign in and open **My orders**. Once an order is dispatched a tracking number
appears next to it, linking to the courier's site. The same number is in your
dispatch email.

## What the statuses mean
- **Processing** — with us, not yet packed. Can still be cancelled.
- **Dispatched** — handed to the courier. Tracking becomes live within a few
  hours.
- **Out for delivery** — on a van today.
- **Delivered** — signed for or left as instructed.

## Tracking has not updated
Courier scans can lag by up to 24 hours, and a parcel dispatched on a Friday
often shows nothing until Monday. If there is no movement for three working days,
contact us with your order number and we will open an enquiry with the courier.

## Marked delivered but nothing arrived
Check with neighbours and look for a card first. If it still cannot be found,
contact us within 14 days of the delivery date so we can claim against the
courier. After 14 days they refuse claims.

## Guest orders
Use the tracking link in your dispatch email. Guest orders do not appear in an
account.
