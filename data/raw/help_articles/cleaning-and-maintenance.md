# Cleaning and maintenance

## Weekly
- **Toasters** — empty the crumb tray, then invert over a sink and shake.
- **Kettles** — empty after use rather than leaving water standing.
- **Grinder** — brush the burr chamber out.

## Monthly
- **Kettles** — descale. See *Descaling your kettle*.
- **Blender** — remove the blade assembly and check the sealing ring.

## Every three months
- **Kettles** — replace the water filter. See *Replacing the water filter*.

## What can go in the dishwasher
- B1 jug and lid: top rack only
- G2 hopper and lid: top rack only
- Toaster crumb trays: yes
- Anything containing a motor, element or electrical contact: **never**

## Cleaning the outside
A damp cloth and washing-up liquid. Never immerse a kettle base, toaster body,
grinder or blender motor unit in water. Avoid abrasive creams and scouring pads
on stainless steel — they leave permanent fine scratches.

## Storage
Let appliances cool and dry fully before putting them away. Do not wrap the flex
tightly around a warm appliance; it stresses the cable where it enters the body.
