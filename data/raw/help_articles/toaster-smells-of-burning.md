# Toaster smells of burning

Applies to the T3 and T5.

## On first use this is normal
New elements carry a protective coating that burns off on the first two or three
cycles. Run the toaster empty on its highest setting twice in a well-ventilated
room before making toast.

## Otherwise it is almost always crumbs
Crumbs collecting on the base char and smoke. Unplug the toaster, empty the
crumb tray, then turn it upside down over a sink and shake it out. Do this
weekly. See *Cleaning and maintenance*.

## Check for anything other than bread
Cheese, butter, and the coating on some frozen pastries drip onto the elements
and smoke persistently. Only plain bread products are suitable.

## A smell of hot plastic or electrical burning
Stop using the toaster and unplug it. This is not a crumb problem. Contact us —
we replace the toaster under the 2-year warranty and ask you to return the old
one so we can inspect it.
