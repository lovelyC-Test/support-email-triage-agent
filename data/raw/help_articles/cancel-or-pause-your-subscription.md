# Cancelling or pausing your subscription

## Cancelling
Sign in, open **My subscriptions**, and choose **Cancel**. It stops immediately
and takes effect at once. There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.

You keep any cartridge already dispatched.

## Pausing instead
**Pause** holds the subscription for up to six months and keeps your price. Use
this if you are away rather than cancelling and restarting.

## Skipping a single delivery
**Skip next delivery** moves the next one on by one interval and leaves the
subscription running. See *Filter subscription terms*.

## If a cartridge has already been dispatched
Cancelling does not recall a parcel in transit. Refuse the delivery for a full
refund, or accept it and return it unopened within 30 days.

## A charge after cancelling
Check the date on the charge against your cancellation confirmation email. A
charge dated before the cancellation is for a cartridge already sent. A charge
dated after it is an error — contact us and we refund it in full.

## Guest orders
Subscriptions require an account and cannot be set up as a guest.
