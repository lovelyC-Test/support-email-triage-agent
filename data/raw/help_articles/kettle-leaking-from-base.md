# Kettle leaking from the base

Applies to the K7 and K7 Pro.

## Stop using it
Unplug the kettle and empty it. Water inside the base is an electrical risk. Do
not put the kettle back on the base until it is completely dry outside.

## Check whether it is a leak or condensation
Boiling water produces steam that condenses on the outside of a cold kettle and
runs down to the base, leaving a small pool. If the pool appears only during
boiling and the kettle does not empty itself overnight, this is condensation and
is normal.

## Check the water gauge seal
On the K7 the gauge is sealed with a rubber gasket that can perish. Fill the
kettle to maximum, stand it on a dry cloth away from the base for ten minutes,
and look for a damp line at the top or bottom of the gauge.

## A genuine leak is a warranty fault
A kettle that loses water while standing has a failed seal or a cracked body and
must not be used. This is covered by the 2-year warranty and we replace rather
than repair. See *How to claim under warranty*.
