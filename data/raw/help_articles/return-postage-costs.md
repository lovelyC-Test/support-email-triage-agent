# Return postage costs

## Free returns
Return postage is free when:

- the item arrived damaged, faulty or incomplete
- we sent the wrong item
- the item developed a fault within the 2-year warranty

In these cases the label we email you is prepaid and there is nothing to pay.

## £3.95 returns
If you are returning an item because you changed your mind, the return label
costs **£3.95**, deducted from your refund. This applies within the standard
30-day window.

## Using your own postage
You may return an item at your own expense using any tracked service. Include
the returns reference inside the parcel. We do not reimburse postage you chose
yourself, and we cannot refund items that arrive without a reference.

## Original delivery charge
The original £3.95 delivery charge is refunded only when the item was faulty or
wrongly sent. It is not refunded when you change your mind.
