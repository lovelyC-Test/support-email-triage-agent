# Spare parts

## What we sell separately
- K7 and K7 Pro: kettle base, mesh filter, filter holder, lid
- T3 and T5: crumb tray
- G2: hopper, lid, upper burr, cleaning brush
- B1: 1.5L jug, lid, tamper, blade assembly, sealing ring

Order these from the **Spare parts** page or by email with your model and the
part name.

## How long parts stay available
We keep parts for **five years** after a model is discontinued.

## Free replacement parts
A part missing from a new delivery is sent free. See *Parts missing from your
delivery*. A part that fails within the 2-year warranty is also free. See *How to
claim under warranty*.

## Parts we will not supply
Anything requiring the appliance to be opened — elements, thermostats, switches,
motors, and mains cables. These are not user-serviceable and we replace the whole
appliance instead.

## Fitting parts yourself
Fitting a part listed above does not affect your warranty. Opening a sealed
appliance does.
