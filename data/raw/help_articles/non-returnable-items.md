# Items that cannot be returned

## Opened filter cartridges
Water filter cartridges cannot be returned once the sealed pouch has been
opened. This is a hygiene rule and applies even if the cartridge has never been
fitted. Unopened cartridges can be returned within the standard 30 days.

## Opened descaler sachets
The same applies to descaler sachets. An unopened box of six can be returned; a
box with any sachet opened cannot.

## Faulty items are always returnable
None of the above applies if the item is faulty. A cartridge that leaks or a
sachet that arrived split can be returned at any time. See *Item damaged on
arrival*.

## Everything else
All appliances — kettles, toasters, the G2 grinder and the B1 blender — are
returnable within 30 days of delivery provided they are unused and in their
original packaging. See *Returns policy*.
