# Kettle will not switch on

Applies to the K7 and K7 Pro.

## Check the kettle is seated on the base
The kettle only receives power when it is sitting squarely on its base. Lift it
off and place it back down; you should feel it settle. If the kettle is slightly
off-centre the contacts do not meet and the switch will not stay down.

## Check the base contacts
Unplug the base and look at the metal contact ring in the centre. Limescale or
spilled liquid on the contacts stops power reaching the kettle. Wipe the ring
with a dry cloth. Do not use water on the base.

## Check the boil-dry cut-out has reset
If the kettle was switched on empty, the boil-dry protection trips and the
kettle will not switch on again until it has cooled. Leave it unplugged and
empty for 20 minutes, then fill to at least the minimum mark and try again.

## Check the socket
Plug another appliance into the same socket to confirm the socket is live. If
you are using an extension lead, try the wall socket directly.

## If none of these work
The switch assembly has most likely failed. This is covered by the 2-year
warranty. See *How to claim under warranty* to arrange a replacement.
