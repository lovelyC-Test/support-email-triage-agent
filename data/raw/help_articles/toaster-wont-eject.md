# Toast will not pop up

Applies to the T3 and T5.

## Unplug it first
Never put a knife, fork or any metal object into a toaster that is plugged in,
even when switched off.

## Check the carriage is not jammed by the bread
Thick or badly cut slices wedge against the guide wires and hold the carriage
down. Unplug, turn the toaster upside down over a sink, and shake gently until
the slice comes free.

## Check the crumb tray is fully closed
The carriage cannot return if the crumb tray is not seated. Slide it fully home
until it clicks.

## Check the cancel button
The carriage releases either on the timer or when cancel is pressed. If cancel
does nothing and the toaster runs to the end of its cycle before releasing, the
cancel switch has failed and the toaster should be replaced under warranty.

## If the carriage will not latch down at all
The magnet in the carriage assembly has lost its hold. This is a warranty fault.
See *How to claim under warranty*.
