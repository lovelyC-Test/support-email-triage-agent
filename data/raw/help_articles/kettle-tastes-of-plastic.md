# Water tastes of plastic

Applies to new K7 and K7 Pro kettles.

## This is normal when new
A new kettle carries a faint plastic taste from the moulding process. It clears
after a few uses.

## Clearing it faster
1. Fill to maximum, boil, and discard the water. Repeat three times.
2. Then descale once using a sachet, even though the kettle is new, and rinse
   thoroughly. See *Descaling your kettle*.

Most customers find the taste gone after this.

## If it persists beyond about ten boils
Check you are not leaving water standing in the kettle between uses — water left
overnight in any plastic-bodied kettle picks up a taste. Empty it after use.

## If it still persists
Contact us. A persistent taste after two weeks of normal use is treated as a
fault and we replace the kettle under the 2-year warranty.
