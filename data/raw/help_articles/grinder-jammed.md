# G2 grinder is jammed

## Unplug it before touching the burrs
The G2 burrs can trap fingers even unpowered.

## Clear the burr chamber
1. Remove the hopper and lid.
2. Twist the upper burr anticlockwise to the release mark and lift it out.
3. Brush both burrs with the cleaning brush supplied.
4. Refit the upper burr and turn clockwise until it seats.

## The usual causes
- **Oily dark-roast beans** compacting in the chute. Grind a small quantity of
  rice to dry the burrs out, then discard it.
- **A stone or bean fragment** wedged between the burrs. Visible once the upper
  burr is out.
- **Grind set too fine for the bean.** See *Choosing a grind setting*.

## Never use water
The burr chamber must stay dry. Water rusts the burrs and voids the warranty.
Use only the brush.

## If the motor hums but the burrs do not turn
The drive coupling has sheared. This is a warranty fault. See *How to claim
under warranty*.
