# Kettle switches off before boiling

Applies to the K7 and K7 Pro.

## Check you are above the minimum mark
Below the minimum fill line the element heats the small volume of water fast
enough that the boil-dry sensor trips before a rolling boil is reached. Fill to
at least the minimum mark.

## Descale it
This is the most common cause. A layer of limescale on the element insulates it,
so the element runs hot while the water stays cool, and the thermal cut-out
trips early. See *Descaling your kettle*.

## Check the lid is fully closed
The steam switch sits in the channel behind the spout and needs a sealed lid to
work correctly. A lid resting slightly open lets steam escape early and the
kettle cuts out before boiling.

## K7 Pro only: check the selected temperature
The K7 Pro stops at the temperature shown on the dial. If the dial reads 80°C the
kettle is working correctly and will not reach a boil. Turn it to 100°C.

## If it still cuts out
The thermostat has failed. This is covered by the 2-year warranty. See *How to
claim under warranty*.
