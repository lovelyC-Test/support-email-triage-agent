# Parts missing from your delivery

## Check the packaging first
Small accessories are often packed under the moulded insert or taped inside the
lid. The filter holder for the K7 Pro and the tamper for the B1 blender are both
packed this way and are easy to miss.

## What should be in the box
- **K7 / K7 Pro** — kettle, base, filter, instructions
- **T3 / T5** — toaster, crumb tray, instructions
- **G2** — grinder, lid, cleaning brush, instructions
- **B1** — motor unit, 1.5L jug, lid, tamper, instructions

## Reporting a missing part
Contact us with your order number and the name of the missing part. We send the
part on its own, free of charge, usually within 2 working days. You do not need
to return the whole appliance.

## If a part is missing and you would rather not wait
Treat it as an incomplete delivery and return the whole item for a full refund.
There is no time limit and postage is free. See *Item damaged on arrival*.
