# Refunds to the original payment method

## We always refund the way you paid
A card payment is refunded to that card. A PayPal payment is refunded to that
PayPal account. This is a fraud-prevention rule and there are no exceptions.

## If your card has expired or been replaced
The refund still works. Your bank routes it to the replacement card
automatically, because the account behind it is unchanged. Allow the usual 5-7
working days.

## If the account is closed
Contact your bank first — most will forward a refund to a nominated account for
a period after closure. If they will not, contact us with your order number and
we will arrange an alternative, which requires additional identity checks and
takes longer.

## Gift orders
The refund goes to the person who paid, not the recipient. We cannot refund a
gift to the recipient's card.
