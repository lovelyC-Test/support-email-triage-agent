# Toast browns unevenly

Applies to the T3 and T5.

## One side darker than the other is expected on the first slice
The elements on each side of a slot reach temperature at slightly different
rates from cold. The first slice of a session is usually paler on one face. From
the second slice onward the toaster is up to temperature and browning evens out.

## Toasting a single slice in a two-slot toaster
Use the slot nearest the control dial. On the T3 and T5 that slot is calibrated
for single-slice use; the other assumes a matching slice opposite it.

## T5 only: the outer slots run cooler
On the four-slot T5 the outer pair sit against the case and lose more heat. For
even results across four slices, set the dial one step higher when using the
outer slots.

## Check for trapped crumbs
A build-up of crumbs against one element reflects heat unevenly. Empty the crumb
tray and turn the toaster upside down over a sink to shake out the rest. See
*Cleaning and maintenance*.

## Bread matters more than the toaster
Fresh, thickly cut, or uneven-density bread browns unevenly in any toaster.
Compare like with like before concluding the toaster is faulty.
