# Payment methods

## What we accept
- Visa, Mastercard and American Express, debit or credit
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- ACE Appliance gift cards

## What we do not accept
Cheques, bank transfers, cash on delivery, and payment over the telephone.

## When you are charged
At the point of order for a single purchase. For subscriptions, on each dispatch.
See *Filter subscription terms*.

## Declined payments
A decline comes from your bank, not from us, and we are not told the reason. Check
the card details and address, then contact your bank. Retrying repeatedly can
trigger a temporary block.

## Pending charges after a failed order
An authorisation can show as pending even when the order did not complete. It
disappears by itself, usually within five working days. No money leaves your
account.

## Refunds
Refunds always return to the method you paid with. See *Refunds to the original
payment method*.

## Gift cards
Redeemable against any product. Not refundable for cash, and expire two years
after purchase.
