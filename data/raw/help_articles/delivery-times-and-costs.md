# Delivery times and costs

## Cost
- **£3.95** standard delivery
- **Free** on orders over £40

## How long
2-3 working days from dispatch for UK mainland addresses. Orders placed before
2pm on a working day are usually dispatched the same day.

Add one working day for the Scottish Highlands, Northern Ireland, the Isle of
Man and the Channel Islands.

## Working days
Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. We do not dispatch or deliver at
weekends.

## Where we deliver
UK addresses only. We cannot ship outside the UK, and we cannot deliver to PO
boxes or BFPO addresses.

## Nobody home
The courier leaves a card and attempts redelivery the next working day. After two
failed attempts the parcel returns to us and we refund the order minus the
original delivery charge.

## Late deliveries
If tracking has not updated for three working days, contact us with your order
number. See *Tracking your order*.
