# What the warranty covers

## Two years from delivery
Every ACE Appliance product carries a **2-year warranty** from the delivery date,
covering faults in materials and manufacture.

## Covered
- An appliance that stops working or never worked
- Failed switches, thermostats, motors and elements
- Perished seals and gaskets
- Cracks that appear without impact

## Not covered
- Accidental damage, drops and knocks
- Limescale damage where the appliance was not descaled. See *Descaling your
  kettle*
- Damage from cleaning products we advise against
- Commercial use
- Normal wear: scratches, discolouration, fading print
- Consumables: filter cartridges and descaler sachets
- Appliances with a cut-off or rewired plug

## Your statutory rights
The warranty is in addition to your legal rights and does not replace them.
