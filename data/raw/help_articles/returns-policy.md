# Returns policy

## Standard returns window
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund. The
item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories.

The 30 days run from the delivery date shown on your order, not the order date.

## Items that arrived damaged or faulty
The 30-day window does not apply to items that arrived damaged, faulty, or
incomplete. See *Damaged on arrival* for how to report these — there is no time
limit, and return postage is always free.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened water filter cartridges and opened descaler sachets cannot be returned
for hygiene reasons unless they are faulty. See *Non-returnable items*.

## Starting a return
Sign in to your account, open the order, and choose **Return an item**. You will
be given a returns reference and a postage label. See *How to return an item*
for the full steps.

## After we receive your return
We inspect returns within 2 working days of arrival. Once approved, your refund
is issued to the original payment method. See *Refund timescales* for how long
the money takes to appear.
