# Registering your product

## You do not have to register
The 2-year warranty applies whether or not you register. Your order number is
proof enough. Registration is a convenience, not a condition.

## What registering gives you
- Warranty claims without needing to find your receipt
- Email notice if a batch is ever recalled
- Reminders when a filter or descale is due

## How to register
Sign in, open **My products**, and choose **Register a product**. You need the
model and the serial number.

## Finding the serial number
- **Kettles** — underside of the base unit
- **Toasters** — underside, next to the crumb tray slot
- **G2 and B1** — underside of the motor unit

It begins with the model code, for example `K7P-` followed by eight digits.

## Bought as a gift
The recipient can register it. A warranty claim still refunds or replaces via the
person who paid. See *Refunds to the original payment method*.
