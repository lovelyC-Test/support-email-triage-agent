# Filter subscription terms

## What you get
One water filter cartridge posted to you every **three months**, at £6.50
including delivery. That is the replacement interval we recommend. See
*Replacing the water filter*.

## When you are charged
On the day each cartridge is dispatched, not in advance. The first charge is at
the same time as your original order.

## Changing the interval
You can move to every two months for hard water areas, or every four, from
**My subscriptions**. The price per cartridge does not change.

## Skipping one delivery
Choose **Skip next delivery** and the schedule resumes afterwards. You are not
charged for a skipped delivery.

## Price changes
We give 30 days' notice by email before any price change, and you can cancel
within that period at the old price.

## No minimum term
There is no contract length and no cancellation fee. See *Cancelling or pausing
your subscription*.

## Returning cartridges
Unopened cartridges can be returned within 30 days. Opened ones cannot. See
*Items that cannot be returned*.
