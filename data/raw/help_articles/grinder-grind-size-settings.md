# Choosing a grind setting on the G2

## The dial
The G2 has 12 settings, 1 being finest.

- **1-2** — espresso
- **3-5** — moka pot, AeroPress
- **6-8** — filter, pour-over
- **9-10** — cafetière
- **11-12** — cold brew

## Adjust while the burrs are turning
Changing setting on a stationary grinder full of beans strains the drive. Start
the grinder, then move the dial.

## If the grind seems inconsistent
Grind a small amount and discard it after changing setting — the chute holds
roughly a spoonful of the previous grind, which mixes with the new one.

## Symptoms of the wrong setting
- Coffee tastes sour or watery: grind finer
- Coffee tastes bitter or the machine struggles to push water through: grind
  coarser
- Grinder stalls or jams: too fine for that bean. See *G2 grinder is jammed*
