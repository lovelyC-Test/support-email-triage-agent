# Contacting us

## Email
**support@aceappliance.example** — the fastest route for anything about an
order, a return, or a fault. We reply within one working day.

Always include your order number. Without it we cannot look anything up, and it
is the single most common reason a reply takes longer.

## What to include
- Order number
- Model, for example K7 Pro or T5
- What is wrong and when it started
- Anything you have already tried

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. We do not monitor email at weekends or on public
holidays.

## What we can help with
Orders, deliveries, returns, refunds, subscriptions, faults, spare parts, and how
to use our products.

## What we cannot help with
- Legal advice
- Repairs to appliances not made by us
- Advice on using our products with equipment we do not supply
- Commercial and catering installations

For anything in that list we will tell you plainly that it is outside what we
cover, rather than guess.
