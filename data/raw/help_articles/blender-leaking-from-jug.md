# B1 jug leaks

## Check the blade assembly is tight
The blade unit screws into the base of the jug and works loose over time,
especially after dishwasher cycles. Empty the jug, hold the blade unit, and turn
it clockwise until firm. Do not overtighten.

## Check the sealing ring is seated and undamaged
Unscrew the blade assembly and lift out the black rubber ring. It must sit flat
in its groove with no twists, nicks or stretched sections. A perished ring is the
most common cause of a leak. Rings are available as a spare part. See *Spare
parts*.

## Check you are not overfilling
The 1.5L jug holds 1.5L cold. Hot liquids expand and lift the lid, so fill to no
more than 1L when blending anything warm.

## Check the jug for cracks
Hold it up to the light and look at the base around the blade housing. A hairline
crack there is a warranty fault; a crack from a knock is not.

## Dishwasher note
The jug is dishwasher safe on the top rack only. The motor unit must never go in
the dishwasher.
