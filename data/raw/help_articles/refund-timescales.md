# Refund timescales

## The stages
1. Your return reaches our warehouse.
2. We inspect it within **2 working days**.
3. We issue the refund on approval and email you to confirm.
4. Your bank or card provider posts the money to your account, which takes a
   further **5-7 working days**.

So allow up to **9 working days** from the day your parcel arrives with us.

## Why the bank stage takes days
Once we issue a refund the money leaves us immediately, but card providers batch
incoming refunds. We cannot speed this up, and neither can our support team.

## If it has been longer than 9 working days
Check the confirmation email to see the date the refund was issued, then contact
your card provider quoting that date. If you never received a confirmation
email, contact us with your order number.

## Refunds always go back the same way
See *Refunds to the original payment method*. We cannot redirect a refund to a
different card or account.
