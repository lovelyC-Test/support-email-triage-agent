# Replacing the plug fuse

## Which fuse
All ACE Appliance products are supplied with a moulded UK three-pin plug fitted
with a **13A fuse**. A replacement must also be 13A and BS 1362 approved.

## Changing it
1. Unplug the appliance.
2. Prise open the fuse carrier on the face of the plug with a small flat
   screwdriver.
3. Lift out the old fuse and fit the new one.
4. Close the carrier until it clicks.

## If the new fuse blows immediately
Stop. A fuse that blows the moment the appliance is switched on indicates a
fault in the appliance, not in the fuse. Do not fit a third fuse or a higher
rating. Contact us — this is covered by the 2-year warranty.

## Extension leads and adaptors
Kettles, toasters and the B1 draw a high current. Plug them directly into a wall
socket. If you must use an extension lead, use a single unwound lead rated at 13A
and do not run two high-current appliances from one lead.

## Cut-off plugs
Never cut the plug off and rewire it. Doing so voids the warranty.
