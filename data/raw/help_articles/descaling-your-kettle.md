# Descaling your kettle

## How often
- Hard water area: **monthly**
- Soft water area: every three months

Limescale is the most common cause of slow boiling, early cut-outs, and white
flakes in the water.

## Using our descaler sachets
1. Empty the kettle.
2. Add one sachet and fill to the maximum mark with cold water.
3. Bring to the boil, then leave to stand for 30 minutes.
4. Pour away, rinse three times, then boil a full kettle of fresh water and
   discard it.

## Using white vinegar instead
Fill halfway with equal parts white vinegar and water, boil, stand for an hour,
then rinse as above. This works but leaves a smell for a few boils.

## Do not use
Bleach, dishwasher tablets, or descaler intended for washing machines. These
damage the element coating and void the warranty.

## The filter
Remove the mesh filter from behind the spout before descaling and rinse it
separately under a running tap. See *Replacing the water filter*.
