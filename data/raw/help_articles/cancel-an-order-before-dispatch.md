# Cancelling an order before it is dispatched

## While the order still says Processing
Sign in, open **My orders**, and choose **Cancel order**. The refund is issued
immediately and no postage is charged. Orders can usually be cancelled for about
two hours after being placed.

## Once the order says Dispatched
It is too late to cancel. The parcel is already with the courier. You have two
options:

- Refuse the delivery when it arrives, and it comes straight back to us
- Accept it and start a normal return within 30 days

Both result in a full refund of the item. Refusing the delivery avoids the £3.95
return postage.

## Subscriptions
Cancelling a single filter delivery is not the same as cancelling the
subscription itself. See *Cancelling or pausing your subscription*.
