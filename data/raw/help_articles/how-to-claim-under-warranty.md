# How to claim under warranty

## What we need
- Your order number, or proof of purchase if bought elsewhere
- The model, for example K7 Pro or T5
- What the fault is and when it started
- Which troubleshooting steps you have already tried

That last point matters. Most reported faults turn out to be limescale or a
seating problem, so we ask you to work through the relevant troubleshooting
article first.

## What happens
1. We confirm the fault is covered, usually within one working day.
2. We email a prepaid return label. Warranty returns are always free to send
   back. See *Return postage costs*.
3. On arrival we inspect within 2 working days.
4. We dispatch a replacement. We replace rather than repair.

## If the same model is discontinued
We supply the nearest current equivalent, or refund at the price you paid if you
prefer.

## If the fault is not covered
We return the appliance and explain why. There is no charge for the inspection,
but you pay return postage in that case.

## Bought from a retailer, not from us
The warranty still applies. Send us the retailer's receipt in place of an order
number.
