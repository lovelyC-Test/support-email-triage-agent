# B1 blender will not start

## Check the jug is locked to the motor unit
The B1 has a safety interlock and will not run until the jug is twisted fully
clockwise into the base. You should hear a click. If the jug is even slightly
out of position the motor receives no power at all.

## Check the lid is on
The interlock also senses the lid. Press it down evenly around the rim until it
seats.

## Check the thermal cut-out has reset
Running the blender for more than 90 seconds continuously trips a thermal
cut-out. Unplug it and leave it for 20 minutes to cool, then try again. For
thick mixtures use short pulses rather than continuous running.

## Check the socket
Plug something else into the same socket to confirm it is live.

## If the motor hums but the blades do not turn
Something is jamming the blades, or the drive coupling has failed. Unplug,
remove the jug, and check the blade assembly turns freely by hand. If it does,
the coupling has gone and the unit is replaced under the 2-year warranty.
